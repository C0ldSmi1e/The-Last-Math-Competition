2. Mathematical result

Four is literally Set Bool, with Mathlib's actual complete Boolean-algebra
structure. CCC is the standard condition that every pairwise-disjoint set of
nonzero elements is countable. It holds since Four is finite. Four has four
elements and has atoms, which the conjecture's bilingual quantifier permits.

Aut B is the full type B ≃o B, equipped with composition and inverse. It is not
all permutations of the four-element carrier, nor a chosen subgroup. Every
order automorphism preserves zero, one, meet, join, complement, and arbitrary
joins; conversely a bijection preserving joins yields an order automorphism.
The file proves every automorphism of Four is either the identity or the
explicit swap of its two atoms. These two are unequal, and an explicit
Aut Four ≃ Bool proves the group's order is exactly two. Mathlib's prime-order
simplicity theorem proves IsSimpleGroup (Aut Four). This uses the conventional
notion of a nontrivial simple group, which includes cyclic prime-order groups;
neither language version requires a nonabelian simple group.

The named comparison objects are genuine carriers:
* RandomAlgebraCarrier is the quotient of Borel subsets of the real line by
  almost-everywhere equality under Lebesgue volume restricted to [0,1]. Class
  equality is proved equivalent to zero measure of symmetric difference.
  Every class has the same class as its Borel representative intersected with
  [0,1]. The measure descends to the quotient. The null class is Boolean zero,
  and the positive-mass classes are precisely the nonzero Borel conditions in
  the problem's positive-measure forcing presentation.
* CohenAlgebraCarrier is the subtype of regular-open subsets of binary Cantor
  space ℕ → Bool, with the standard discrete/product topology. Regular openness
  is the actual equation interior (closure U) = U. Each coordinate-true cylinder
  is clopen and hence regular open; distinct coordinates give distinct sets.

For random, the five intervals [0,i/4], i∈Fin 5, give distinct classes by their
measures 0, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1. For Cohen, five coordinate cylinders are distinct.
A carrier with an injection from Fin 5 cannot have a bijection with Four.
Thus Four cannot be isomorphic to either named algebra, since any such
isomorphism has an underlying equivalence.
